# Case Studies and Engineering Precedents

## Purpose
Use public engineering experience to reduce avoidable rediscovery while preserving context and limitations.

## Research rules
1. Prefer primary sources: engineering blogs, handbooks, postmortems, technical reports, standards and maintained repositories.
2. Record source URL, author/organisation, publication date and access date.
3. Separate reported facts from our interpretation.
4. Capture failures and trade-offs as well as successes.
5. Do not infer causation from correlation or universalise one team's practice.
6. Check source licences before copying text, diagrams, code or datasets.
7. Link to external repositories rather than importing their source/history wholesale.
8. Revisit entries when sources change.

## Further STaaS/business intelligence work
A separate dataset register should capture provenance, geography, sector, company stage, funding, operating model, failures, pivots, acquisitions, outcomes, licence, update frequency, quality and evidence gaps. A target of 100–200 deeply researched cases is a research goal, not a claim that the corpus exists.
